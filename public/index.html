    No Mercy rules

    • Match color, number, or symbol. No match? Draw until you can play — then you must play it.
    • Stacking: answer a +2 / +4 / +6 / +10 with an equal or higher draw card. Whoever can’t takes the whole pile.
    • Challenge: if a +2 / +4 / +6 / +8 / +10 is played on you, hit Challenge. If they still had the previous color, they draw that amount and you keep your turn. If they didn’t, you draw that amount +2.
    • 7: you must swap hands with a player of your choice.
    • 0: everyone passes their hand in the current direction.
    • Discard All: dump every card of that color.
    • Skip Everyone: skip the table and play again.
    • Wild Color Roulette: the next player picks a color, then reveals cards until they hit it (wilds don’t count).
    • 10: play again immediately.
    • Wild Reverse +8: reverse, then the next player draws 8 (stackable).
    • Wild Discard All: pick a color and dump every card of that color.
    • Wild Final Attack: next player draws 1 per action/wild in your hand. 7+ actions: they draw 25 and everyone else draws 5.
    • Wild Sudden Death: every player draws until they have 24 cards.
    • Coin: Mercy redraws 7. No Mercy doubles the next draw card you play. Once per game.
    • Mercy: 25 or more cards in hand and you’re eliminated.
    • Down to one card? Hit UNO! before someone catches you, or draw 2.
    • Win by emptying your hand — or by knocking everyone else out.
